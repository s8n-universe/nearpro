## 🎬 TECHNICAL PRE-RECORDING SETUP & BEST PRACTICES

Before recording any screen footage for NearPro, ensure your recording environment meets these professional production standards:

### 1. Recording Software & Canvas Settings
- **Resolution**: 1920×1080 (1080p Full HD) or 3840×2160 (4K UHD).
- **Frame Rate**: 60 FPS (for ultra-smooth cursor movement and modal transitions).
- **Software Recommended**: OBS Studio, ScreenFlow, Camtasia, or Loom (High Quality Mode).
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 widescreen.

### 2. Browser Environment Hygiene
- **Browser**: Google Chrome in Fullscreen / App Mode (`F11` or Clean Window).
- **Zoom Level**: Exactly 100% (default viewport scaling).
- **Clean Interface**: 
  - Hide Chrome Bookmarks Bar (`Ctrl+Shift+B` or `Cmd+Shift+B`).
  - Disable or hide all browser extension icons.
  - Clear browser cache and history prior to recording for clean data loads.
  - Disable browser notifications (Do Not Disturb mode ON).

### 3. Cursor & Audio Controls
- **Cursor Effects**: Enable a soft halo yellow/gold cursor highlight (`#ffa000`) with smooth click ripple animation.
- **Audio Voiceover**: 
  - Condenser microphone (e.g., Shure SM7B, Elgato Wave:3, or Rode NT-USB).
  - Sample Rate: 48 kHz / 24-bit.
  - Background Noise Suppression & De-Esser applied.

**Primary Goal**: High-energy teaser & product overview for social media ads, landing page hero video, and LinkedIn announcements.

  - Select Area: `Bandra` / `Andheri West`.
  - Show the 4-step Agency Growth Architecture card on the Getting Started screen.
- **Voiceover Instructions**:
  > *"In this video, we'll walk through how an agency founder uses NearPro to discover, audit, and close a 30,000-rupee monthly client in under 10 minutes. First, log into your NearPro workspace and set your target category and location..."*

### Step 4: Executing WhatsApp Outreach & Teleprompter Call Script (2:00 - 2:30)
- **Visual Action**:
  - Click **"Outreach Studio"**.
  - Show the WhatsApp message template populated with `{lead_name}`, `{area}`, and the audit PDF link.
  - Demonstrate clicking **"Send via WhatsApp"** (or opening Web WhatsApp).

  > *"Now it's time to reach out. Click Outreach Studio to launch a pre-formatted Hinglish WhatsApp message with the audit attached. If you prefer phone outreach, use the Sales Teleprompter to guide your call and handle objections without hesitating."*

  - Show the Kanban board columns (`New`, `Contacted`, `Pitched`, `Closed Retainer`).

- **Voiceover Instructions**:
  > *"Finally, track every stage of the deal in your integrated B2B CRM. Set follow-up reminders, add client notes, and drag closed leads into 'Closed Retainer'. Everything stays organized in one place."*

## 🛠️ POST-PRODUCTION & EDITING CHECKLIST

1. **Callout Highlights & Magnification**:
   - Add a subtle 1.2× zoom effect on key UI elements when mentioned (e.g., "No Website" badge, PDF Download button, WhatsApp trigger).
2. **On-Screen Text Badges**:
   - Add clean lower-third graphics:
     - `✦ STEP 1: Discover Gap Leads`
     - `✦ STEP 2: Generate 3-Page Audit`
     - `✦ STEP 3: 1-Click WhatsApp Outreach`
     - `✦ STEP 4: Close Retainer in CRM`
3. **Background Music**:
   - Soft, upbeat corporate tech background track (Lo-Fi Beats or Corporate Tech Ambient) at -22dB volume behind voiceover.
4. **Export Settings**:
   - Codec: H.264 / MP4.
   - Bitrate: 15–20 Mbps (for crystal-clear text and zero blur).
   - Audio: AAC 320 kbps.

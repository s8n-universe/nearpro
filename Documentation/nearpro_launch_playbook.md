## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PRODUCT SHOWCASE BLUEPRINT

### What is NearPro?
NearPro is **India’s First Trusted B2B Lead Intelligence Platform & Autonomous Agency Operating System**. It is built specifically for freelancers, agency owners, B2B sales professionals, digital marketers, and local business consultants.

### The Problem We Solve
Every freelancer and agency in India spends 15+ hours every week executing a disjointed, manual client acquisition process:
1. Searching Google Maps / JustDial / IndiaMART line by line.
2. Manually copying business names, phone numbers, and addresses into Excel spreadsheets.
3. Manually inspecting client websites to see if they are slow, unoptimized, or missing SSL.
4. Typing generic, cold WhatsApp messages or emails one by one.
5. Spending hours formatting proposal PDFs and guessing tele-sales pitches.
6. Losing track of follow-ups because leads live in fragmented sticky notes or WhatsApp chats.

### The NearPro Solution (The Unified 360° Workstation)
NearPro collapses the entire 6-step agency workflow into a single, seamless workstation:
- **Up-to-Date Verified Local Business Data**: Access thousands of verified business leads (currently Mumbai, expanding to Pune & Delhi) cross-verified for high trust.
- **1-Click Business Health Check (`#/dashboard/audit`)**: Audit any business website in real-time for mobile-friendliness, PageSpeed, SEO meta tags, and conversion gaps.
- **AI Multi-Channel Outreach Studio (`#/dashboard/outreach`)**: Instantly generate personalized cold outreach messages in **Hinglish** or **English** tailored to the client's specific rating, location, and website status, with 1-click WhatsApp launch.
- **3-Page PDF Proposal Generator (`#/dashboard/proposals`)**: Draft bespoke agency proposals with customizable pricing tiers and export to PDF or share via public link (`#/d/:id`).
- **Tele-Sales Teleprompter Scripts (`#/dashboard/call-scripts`)**: Generate cold call scripts with gatekeeper bypasses, hooks, and objection handlers.
- **360° AI Deal Workstation (`#/dashboard/crm`)**: Track every lead from **New Lead → Contacted → In Progress → Closed Won → Closed Lost** with follow-up reminders.

## APPENDIX: EMERGENCY INCIDENT RESPONSE PLAYBOOK

### 1. Database Spike / Connection Limit Exceeded
- Action: Upgrade Supabase to Pro Tier ($25/mo) in Dashboard → Billing (takes 2 minutes, no code migration needed).

### 2. Razorpay Webhook Payment Delay
- Action: Manually verify transaction ID in Razorpay dashboard and toggle `is_premium = true` in Supabase `public.profiles` table.

### 3. Data Inaccuracy Complaint
- Action: Inspect `inaccuracy_reports` table, update or soft-delete record in `professionals` table, and send email response to user within 12 hours.

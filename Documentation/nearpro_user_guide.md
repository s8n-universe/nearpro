## 1. INTRODUCTION & QUICK START GUIDE

Welcome to **NearPro** — India’s #1 Autonomous B2B Lead Intelligence Operating System. NearPro collapses the entire 6-step agency workflow into a single, unified workstation:

```
Search Leads ➔ Run 1-Click Audit ➔ Draft AI Outreach ➔ Generate PDF Proposal ➔ Execute Call Script ➔ Close Deal in CRM
```

### Quick 3-Step Start:
1. **Login to Dashboard**: Access your workspace dashboard at `https://nearpro.s8n.in`.
2. **Set Profile Preferences**: Open **Profile Settings** and configure your **Caller Gender** (Male/Female) for natural cold calling verb inflections.
3. **Save Your First Lead**: Search Dentists or Real Estate in Bandra, click **Track Lead**, and assign it to a Smart List.

## 2. MODULE 1: VERIFIED B2B LEAD DIRECTORY

The Directory contains **12,358+ verified business profiles** across Mumbai (expanding to Pune & Delhi/NCR).

### How to Use:
- **Niche Filter**: Filter by Healthcare, Real Estate, Legal, Beauty/Salon, Dental, Tech, or Local Services.
- **Location Filter**: Narrow down by micro-location (e.g., Bandra West, Andheri East, Lower Parel).
- **Ratings & Data Completeness**: Instantly see Google rating, review count, address, direct phone number, and website status.
- **Leaflet Map View**: Toggle to Map View to see geographic lead density pin clusters.
- **Inline Smart List Creation (`+`)**: Click the **Track Lead** button on any business card to save the lead to a smart list or click the `+` icon to instantly create a new list (e.g. *Hot Leads*, *Closed Deals*).

## 3. MODULE 2: 1-CLICK BUSINESS HEALTH CHECK

Audit any business website in real-time to uncover page load bottlenecks and mobile conversion gaps before reaching out.

### How to Use:
1. Paste any client website URL (e.g., `https://example-dentist.com`).
2. Click **Run Health Check**.
3. **Audit Output**:
   - **PageSpeed Score (0-100)**: Mobile load time in seconds.
   - **SEO Meta Inspection**: Title tag, meta description, and H1 tag validation.
   - **Estimated Revenue Loss**: Calculates annual lost revenue caused by slow mobile load speeds.
   - **1-Click Outreach Pitch**: Click "Pitch Optimization Service" to send audit results directly into the Outreach Studio.

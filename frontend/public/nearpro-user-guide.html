🚀 1. Introduction & 3-Step Quick Start

Welcome to NearPro — India's #1 Autonomous B2B Lead Intelligence Operating System. NearPro collapses the entire 6-step agency client acquisition workflow into a single unified workstation:

Search Leads ➔ Run 1-Click Audit ➔ Draft AI Outreach ➔ Generate PDF Proposal ➔ Execute Call Script ➔ Close Deal in CRM

Quick 3-Step Setup:

  1. Access Workspace: Open your dashboard at https://nearpro.s8n.in/#/dashboard/directory.
  2. Set Profile Preferences: Open Profile Settings and select your Caller Gender (Male/Female) for natural cold call verb inflections.
  3. Track Your First Lead: Filter Dentists or Real Estate in Bandra, click Track Lead, and save it to a Smart List.

📍 2. Verified B2B Lead Directory (#/dashboard/directory)

The Directory contains 12,358+ verified business profiles across Mumbai (expanding to Pune & Delhi/NCR).

  • Niche & Micro-Location Filters: Narrow down leads by category (Healthcare, Real Estate, Legal, Dental, Beauty, Tech) and neighborhood (Bandra, Andheri, Lower Parel).
  • Google Ratings & Data Completeness: Instantly view review scores, direct phone numbers, and website URLs.
  • Leaflet Map View: Toggle to geographic map view to see density pin clusters.
  • Inline Smart List Creation (+): Click Track Lead to assign leads to existing lists or click the + icon to create custom lists (e.g., Hot Leads, Closed Deals) in one click.

⚡ 3. 1-Click Business & PageSpeed Health Check (#/dashboard/audit)

Audit any prospect's website in real time to reveal performance bottlenecks before making first contact.

  • Mobile PageSpeed Score (0-100): Measures exact mobile load times.
  • SEO Meta Inspection: Checks title tags, meta descriptions, and H1 heading structure.
  • Revenue Drop-Off Calculator: Estimates annual lost client revenue caused by slow mobile load speeds.
  • 1-Click Pitch Export: Click "Pitch Optimization Service" to push audit findings directly into the AI Outreach Studio.

💬 4. Multi-Channel AI Outreach Studio (#/dashboard/outreach)

Draft high-converting personalized pitch copy tailored to prospect rating, location, and audit results.

  • Language Selector: Toggle between conversational Hinglish or formal English.
  • Channel Formats: Tailored templates for WhatsApp, Cold Email, and LinkedIn Message.
  • 1-Click WhatsApp Launch: Opens WhatsApp Web instantly with pre-filled personalized message copy.

📄 5. 3-Page Agency PDF Proposals (#/dashboard/proposals)

Auto-generate professional 3-page pitch decks tailored for prospects, ready for PDF export or public link sharing.

  • Package Tiering: Choose Starter, Growth, or Enterprise retainer pricing.
  • Public View Links: Share live proposal URLs (https://nearpro.s8n.in/#/d/:id) directly with decision-makers.
  • PDF Export: Download high-resolution print-ready PDFs formatted with your agency branding.

📞 6. AI Tele-Sales Teleprompter Scripts (#/dashboard/call-scripts)

Execute cold calls with live teleprompter scripts and real-time objection handling cards.

  • Gender Inflection Engine: Automatically adapts Hindi/Hinglish verbs based on your Profile Gender setting ("bol raha/rahi hoon", "karta/karti hoon").
  • Objection Cards: 1-click objection handling cards for common gatekeeper responses ("Send an email", "No budget", "Already have an agency").

🚀 7. Antigravity AI System Prompt Generator (#/dashboard/prompts)

Build comprehensive web architecture system prompts tailored for modern AI platforms (Antigravity AI, Vite, Next.js).

  • Dynamic Progress Bar: Animated 0% to 100% progress counter while prompts are generated.
  • Sticky Top-Right Copy Button: Sticky copy button anchored inside the prompt box container for effortless copying.

📊 8. 360° AI Deal Workstation & CRM (#/dashboard/crm)

Manage deals across a 5-column Kanban pipeline with dynamic valuation and activity heatmaps.

  • 5 Kanban Columns: 🔵 New Leads ➔ 📞 Contacted ➔ 💬 Offer Sent ➔ 🏆 Converted ➔ 🔒 Closed.
  • Dynamic Deal Valuation: Deal values calculated by niche metrics (Real Estate ₹75k, Legal ₹60k, Healthcare ₹50k, Dental ₹40k, Beauty ₹25k, Default ₹30k).
  • 14-Day Activity Heatmap: Visual grid tracking your actual daily lead engagement over the last 14 days.
